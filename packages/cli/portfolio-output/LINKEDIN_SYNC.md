# Two Birds, One Stone: Sync Your LinkedIn & Launch Your Portfolio

Recruiters and collaborators almost always check both your **LinkedIn** and your **Portfolio**. Use this checklist right after deploying **https://hellonigga.github.io** so both profiles tell the exact same story.

---

## 1. Update Your LinkedIn Headline
Click the **pencil icon** on your LinkedIn top profile card and paste this Headline:

```text
Electronics & Systems Engineer | RTL Design & Embedded Systems
```

---

## 2. Update Your LinkedIn "About" Section
Make sure the first 3 lines hook the reader before LinkedIn's *"see more"* fold:

```text
I enjoy building reliable hardware-software systems from first principles—from digital logic and bus protocols to clean developer tooling.

Currently: Open to Graduate Research Opportunities and Hardware/Systems Engineering roles.

Explore my projects and work here: https://hellonigga.github.io
```

---

## 3. Align Experience, Education & Top Skills
- **Experience & Education:** Verify that your role titles, organizations, and dates match what you entered in `portfolio.json`.
- **Top Skills to Pin on LinkedIn:** Digital Logic, SystemVerilog, Computer Architecture, RTL Verification, Linux / WSL, Git & GitHub, Python, Bash / Zsh
- **Contact Info:** Click **Contact info** on your LinkedIn profile -> **Edit** -> add **https://hellonigga.github.io** as your Personal Website.

---

## 4. Pin Your Portfolio to LinkedIn "Featured" (High Visibility!)
Make your live website impossible to miss on your LinkedIn profile:
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile and click **Add profile section** -> **Recommended** -> **Add featured**.
2. Click the **+** icon and choose **Add a link**.
3. Paste: `https://hellonigga.github.io`
4. Set Title to: `Alex Sharma — Portfolio & Projects`
5. Click **Save**. It will now appear visually near the top of your LinkedIn profile!

---

## 5. Publish Your LinkedIn Launch Post (Ready-to-Copy Draft)
Share your new portfolio with your network so people actually click it:

```text
Excited to share my new personal portfolio website! 🚀

I put together a clean home for my projects, background, and what I'm currently building:
- Custom AXI4 DMA Controller: High-throughput memory-to-memory DMA engine built from scratch in SystemVerilog.
- 8-Bit Breadboard Microcomputer: Discrete logic CPU with custom ALU, control unit, and instruction set.

Take a look here: https://hellonigga.github.io

Always open to feedback, ideas, and connecting with folks working in similar areas!

#Portfolio #Projects #LearningInPublic #Career
```
